Databricks to Microsoft Fabric production migration

Problem
A financial reporting platform had to move off Databricks to Fabric without breaking the Power BI reports the business relied on.
Approach
Moved legacy datasets into Fabric Lakehouse, Warehouse and OneLake with Fabric Data Pipelines. Added CDC from core banking databases and automated Dev/Test/Prod promotion with Fabric deployment pipelines and Azure DevOps.
Result
Production cutover with full historical data integrity. Direct Lake semantic models were re-pointed at cutover, refresh latency dropped 32%, and manual promotion errors were eliminated.

Automotive · BI
18s → under 6s

Making leadership dashboards fast again

Problem
Power BI semantic models on top of the warehouse took about 18 seconds per query, slowing down the reports leadership checks every day.
Approach
Profiled the slow queries, then redesigned the serving layer as star-schema fact and dimension tables with SCD Type 1 and 2 in dbt across Azure Synapse and Snowflake.
Result
Query time fell from 18 seconds to under 6, measured with SQL query profiling, on the models leadership uses every day.
