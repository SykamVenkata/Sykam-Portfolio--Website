Case 01 · Financial services

Databricks to Microsoft Fabric production migration

Cutover with full historical data integrity
Problem
A financial reporting platform had to move off Databricks to Fabric without breaking the Power BI reports the business relied on.

Case 02 · Automotive BI

Making leadership dashboards fast again

Power BI queries from 18s to under 6s
Problem
Power BI semantic models on top of the warehouse took about 18 seconds per query, slowing down the reports leadership checks every day.
Approach
Profiled the slow queries, then redesigned the serving layer as star-schema fact and dimension tables with SCD Type 1 and 2 in dbt across Azure Synapse and Snowflake.
Result
Query time fell from 18 seconds to under 6, measured with SQL query profiling.

Featured · Streaming Lakehouse

Real-time vehicle telemetry Lakehouse

A simulated fleet streams readings into Event Hubs. Databricks Structured Streaming lands them in Delta Lake Bronze, Silver and Gold tables with data quality rules, deduplication and late-data handling, Unity Catalog governs access, and Power BI shows the fleet live.

  • 13 data quality rules, with failed rows kept in a quarantine table

  • Terraform and a Databricks Asset Bundle for Azure deployment
